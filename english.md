## Research {#research-zhengshen}

- **Bridging Data Silos in Cost Estimating: An Agent-Augmented Federated Learning Approach**  
  *First author*  
  Sept. 2025 - Aug. 2026  
  Developed a horizontal federated-learning setting using Florida highway project data and three collaborating clients. Introduced a multi-agent aggregation mechanism that adjusts client weights based on training states. Experiments reported a **6.81% reduction in MAPE relative to FedAvg**.

- **Deep Representation Learning for Ecological Risk Topic Modeling in Major Engineering Projects** — Accepted by *Journal of Guangzhou University*  
  *Core researcher; National Undergraduate Innovation Project*  
  Jun. 2025 - May 2026  
  Built a **BGE-M3–UMAP–KMeans–BERTopic** pipeline to analyze ecological-risk literature. Conducted LDA benchmarking, HDBSCAN sensitivity analysis, and manual review; identified **eight risk themes** and established **24 traceable evidence chains** for risk identification, source tracing, and governance.

- **AI-Enabled Refined Operations and Maintenance Management for Engineering Projects**  
  *Core researcher; University Undergraduate Innovation Project; completed*  
  Jun. 2025 - Mar. 2026  
  Developed an intelligent operations and maintenance platform using embedding models, **FAISS**, and **RAG**. Built a construction-safety knowledge base from **53,550 accident records**, with hybrid retrieval, evidence constraints, and output validation to support traceable risk assessment.
